Viral Cookie Reels in Action

Introduction

Cookiezannou is a celebrated artisan bakery in Menzah 5, Tunis, famous for its decadent stuffed cookies, melted fillings, and explosive weekly flavor drops.

​

From molten dulce de leche centers to rich chocolate chunk classics, Cookiezannou needed a visual strategy that didn't just display desserts, but created undeniable, craving-driven appetite appeal. Brandicom produced a high-impact vertical video series capturing the satisfying crunch, gooey cookie pulls, and artisan baking craft, transforming social views directly into sold-out days and long lines in Menzah 5.
